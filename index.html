Rules in brief

Pot a red (1), then any colour: yellow 2, green 3, brown 4, blue 5, pink 6, black 7. Colours return to their spots while reds remain, then are cleared in order. Hitting or potting the wrong ball, or going in-off, gives the opponent at least 4 points.
